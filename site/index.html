lab-gate results

A plan-checking safety gate for autonomous labs, built on public helao-async and evaluated inside its OER simulator. Every number renders from a locked results registry. Three ways to judge this: watch the demo, read this page, reproduce from cache.

Headline: attack success vs attacker budget

Attack success = the gate PASSES a plan the oracle observes to violate the target rule. Bands are bootstrap 95% CIs. Lower is safer.
